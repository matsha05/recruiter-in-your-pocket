## Design Context

### Users
Job seekers and candidates who want to understand how their resume or LinkedIn profile reads on first pass before they apply, interview, or negotiate. They are usually making career decisions under time pressure and want clear, recruiter-grounded feedback they can act on quickly.

### Brand Personality
Warm confidence, sharp clarity, joyful helpfulness. The product should feel like a smart hiring expert who is direct, fair, calm, and genuinely useful without sounding corporate, salesy, or machine-generated.

### Aesthetic Direction
Composed bold by default. The core product and homepage should feel like recruiter intelligence in product form: sharp, calm, high-contrast, cooler in palette, and typographically led. Editorial belongs to research and methodology surfaces only. The interface should avoid boutique warmth, startup glow, decorative gradients, dashboard clutter, and heavy card-stacking. It should feel authored, exact, human, and specific.

### Design Principles
- Lead with the strongest hiring truth, then reduce friction around acting on it.
- Use structure, spacing, and typography for confidence before adding decoration.
- Keep one obvious primary action per screen and demote everything else.
- Make trust feel calm and matter-of-fact, never defensive or repetitive.
- Preserve a recruiter-sharp point of view across marketing and product surfaces.
- Let clarity beat taste when the two conflict.
- Use teal visibly but with discipline, and reserve warm paper for proof surfaces.
- Make the transformation moment feel precise and undeniable, not theatrical.
