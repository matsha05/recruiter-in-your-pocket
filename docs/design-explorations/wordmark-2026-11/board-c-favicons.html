A favicon for Two Voices

Two Voices is the pick, and a plain R is too generic for it. Each option below puts the recruiter’s Literata italic, in the green pen, next to or inside the product’s Schibsted R. The 16 px versions are redrawn on whole pixels rather than scaled down, and every file comes in light (paper tile) and dark (ink tile).

C1 · The Finished R
C2 · The Noted R
C3 · R in

Recommendation: C1, The Finished R. It is the only option where the two voices make one shape, the way the italic “in” sits inside the name, and it still reads as an R in a 16 px tab.

Option C1 · Recommended

The Finished R

Schibsted’s R with its leg written in Literata italic, in the green pen. The product sets the letter and the recruiter finishes it: both voices in one shape, the same move as the italic “in” in the logotype.

Option C2

The Noted R

The R, underlined once in the recruiter’s pen, with italic-cut ends and a slight lift at the finish. The crispest at 16 px and the closest to today’s favicon, but the stroke says less about two voices than C1 does.

Option C3

R in

The R and the italic “in” side by side: the first two words of the name in their two voices. The most literal link to the logotype, but at 16 px the italic turns into upright pixels and the pair reads as “Rin”.

Set aside: the italic “in” on its own. It is the prettiest at 180 px, but a rounded tile that says “in” reads as LinkedIn in exactly the place job seekers keep LinkedIn open. Files: docs/design-explorations/wordmark-2026-11/c-two-voices/favicons/.
