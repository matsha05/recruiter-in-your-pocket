Direction A

The Marked Line

The symbol is the product: a resume sheet with the one line a recruiter would miss swiped in highlighter, so the logo teaches what the yellow means before the report does. It is the most literal of the three and the closest to the wedge, and it puts the highlighter on every page, which is also its cost.

Site header at 1440 px (real size on a desktop screen). Logotype set at 20 px beside 15 px navigation.

Direction B

The Pocket Pen

The name, drawn: the recruiter's green pen clipped in your shirt pocket, the tool a recruiter always has on them, and now so do you. It spends no yellow, explains the name at a glance, and gives the favicon a silhouette nobody in the category owns; it is the most charming of the three and the least literal about what the product does.

Site header at 1440 px (real size on a desktop screen). Logotype set at 20 px beside 15 px navigation.

Direction C

Two Voices

The name is the logo: Schibsted for the product, and a Literata italic "in" in the green pen, the same two voices as every report, so a long name reads as two easy halves. It is the quietest change and the safest at every size; the favicon falls back to a plain R, which is honest but the least distinctive symbol here.

Site header at 1440 px (real size on a desktop screen). Logotype set at 21 px beside 15 px navigation.
