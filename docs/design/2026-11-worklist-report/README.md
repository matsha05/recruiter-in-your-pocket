# The worklist report: direction sketch, November 5, 2026

Two low-effort shapes for the report, sketched on Sam Whitaker's synthetic resume after the judge v5 blind pick (`tests/fixtures/results/blind-pick-v5/`), where Matt said four times he was not sold on the Critical Miss framing. `sketch.html` holds both; `shape2.png` is the one he chose.

## What he chose

**Shape 2: the write-up first, then one card per line.**

- Score and rung at the top, as now.
- The recruiter's write-up: two or three paragraphs in the pen's voice, "not a forced 3 sentence summary".
- Then the lines that need work, in the order a recruiter hits them, one card each. A card is: the line as written, what a recruiter can't tell from it, an example of the line with the detail in (blanks the person fills, in the candidate's blue, nothing invented as fact), and the question that fills the blank. He confirmed all three parts.
- Structural items (section order, dates, a gap) are cards too. Lines that are fine aren't listed, or one gets a "keep as is" card as the model for the rest.
- No Critical Miss label anywhere. Strong resumes get fewer cards and a shorter write-up.

His bar, verbatim: "it needs the same UI polish a top product designer or engineer would appreciate. we can't get this wrong. and the copy/tone has to be great. some wit, human warmth, the stuff we defined before. not robot ai slop."

## What is not decided

- The public copy that names the Critical Miss (homepage, pricing, methodology, help) and the "up to three changes" lines.
- What the judge measures once there is no single Critical Miss to compare.
- Names: he rejected "the read" as a label. The write-up and the cards have no product names yet.
