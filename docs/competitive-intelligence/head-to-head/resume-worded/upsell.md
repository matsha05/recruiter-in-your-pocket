# Upsell capture

`upsell.png` captures the first signed-in dashboard upgrade offer, before the completed scoring upload. It advertises “PRO + LINKEDIN OPTIMIZATION” and describes the LinkedIn add-on as “worth $49.” This is a bonus valuation, not a subscription charge. LinkedIn was not run.

`first-plan-price.png` captures the first actual subscription-price screen after opening the baseline Pro offer: Monthly **$49/month**, Quarterly **$33/month**, One Year **$19/month**, with **$75** crossed out for each. The screen states prices are USD. Monthly was selected by default. No Buy/checkout button was clicked and no card was entered.

The baseline report showed ten locked PRO checks. Targeted Resume showed five locked Magic Target recommendations after one free recommendation. Their visible upgrade text is recorded in `recommendations.md`; unseen content was not inferred.
