# What the AI engines say: November 2026

The first run of the twelve-prompt set from [the plan](../2026-11-aeo-and-internal-linking.md), phase 3. One file per month, same prompts, same columns, so the change is readable month over month. The number that matters is how many of the twelve answers per engine name or cite Recruiter in Your Pocket.

**This month is not a baseline.** Only four of the twelve ChatGPT answers could be read; the other eight failed to load in the interface. Of the four, none named or cited RIYP. The eight need to be rerun before this file can say 0 of 12.

## Method

Run on November 5, 2026 by ChatGPT (Astra 6.1) working from [the shared brief](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1bgeI0jjwulSXQBpvOvFdn3igzqCPxDguYHRsKSDeDwc/edit), section 7 of which holds every cited URL in answer order. One fresh, unpersonalized temporary chat per prompt, web search on, the prompt verbatim, nothing added. Where the engine asked a clarifying question (prompts 9 and 11) the reply was "just the general case" and both answers were captured. The interface showed "High" effort and no model name. No other engine was run: Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Mode need Matt's accounts.

"Tools named" lists products the answer recommends or discusses, in order; a publisher that appears only as a cited source is marked as such. "Sources" lists the cited domains in order; the full URLs are in the doc.

## What the four answers show

- **The sources are vendors and their roundups.** Prompt 11 cited twenty-four links and every one was a resume tool's own site, pricing page or "best tools" post (Teal, Jobscan, Enhancv, Acedit). That matches the Parse tracker's picture and the plan's premise: the engines name the tools that vendor roundups name, and the roundups are written by the vendors ranking themselves first.
- **Prompt 12 did not reach a study.** The answer about the first pass cited employer guidance (Indeed, Greenhouse, LinkedIn) and nothing about how recruiters actually read. That is the question [how-recruiters-read](https://www.recruiterinyourpocket.com/research/how-recruiters-read) answers with Pina et al. 2023 and the 2012 eye-tracking report. The article is not being found for its own question; whether that is indexing lag (the cross-links went live November 4) or a title mismatch, a rerun in December will tell.
- **Prompt 10 leans on one vendor's test.** HiredRadar's eight-tool test is the evidence ChatGPT used for whether checkers are accurate. HiredRadar accepts tool submissions; it is on the [outreach tracker](../outreach-tracker.md).
