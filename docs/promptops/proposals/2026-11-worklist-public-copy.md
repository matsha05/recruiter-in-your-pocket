# The public copy that names the old unit

Status: proposed, November 6, 2026. Nothing here changes a public page until Matt decides each line. The worklist report (score and rung, the write-up, one card per line) replaced the Critical Miss report in the product on November 6 (`docs/audits/2026-11-worklist-run.md`); these are the public lines that still describe the old unit, with a proposed replacement for each. The in-product labels (the cards, the headings, the PDF) are already the new ones; they are recorded in `docs/copy-system.md`, "The worklist report".

Principles behind the proposals: say what the report does in the reader's words (the lines a recruiter trips on, in order, with what to add), never "the read" or "Critical Miss", no count promised ("up to three" is gone: a weak page gets every line that needs obvious work, a strong page gets few), and no interview-cost claim on any line, since the cards aren't a pass/fail unit.
