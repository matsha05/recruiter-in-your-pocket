# The worklist's own copy, read aloud

November 6, 2026. Matt, after shipping the worklist's typography: "we really need to think hard about the copy and the tone. e.g. 'line by line, in the order a recruiter hits them' wut?" This is every product string on the report surface (not the model's words: the verdict, the write-up, the notes, the questions and the examples are the prompt's, and Matt said he loves the content), read against `docs/voice-and-tone.md`, with the change and the reason.

The test applied to each line (the voice guide's checklist): would an experienced recruiter say this sentence to a candidate; does it name the actual subject or make the reader translate a product word ("the read", "the page", "rung", "card"); is the button literal; is the one aside on the screen earned.

| Where | Was | Now | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The list's heading | Line by line, in the order a recruiter hits them. | **Lines that need work.** with the lead **Top to bottom, the way a recruiter reads.** | "Hits them" is our jargon and the heading had no subject. The heading names what the list is; the lead carries the order in plain words. The tab stays `Lines`, the short form of the heading. |
| The list's heading, clean page | Nothing here needs work. | unchanged | Says the thing. |
| The keep entry's label | Keep as is / The model for the rest | **Don't touch this one** / **Write the others like it.** | The voice guide's own reference line for a strength to leave alone. "The model for the rest" asked the reader to translate. |
| Leaving a blank | Left for now. A blank reads as a question, and recruiters don't call to ask it. | **Left blank for now. A recruiter will have the same question, and they won't call to ask it.** | Names who has the question. Still the one aside on the entry, and still a hiring truth. |
| Filling it after all | Fill it in after all | unchanged | Literal, and warm enough. |
| Copy | Copy the line / Copying / Copied | unchanged | Literal. |
| Under a filled line | Every fact in it is from your resume or your answers. | unchanged | The facts rule, said plainly. |
| Kept answers | Kept with this report. / Keeping this with your report… / We couldn't keep this with your report just now. Copy the line to be safe. | unchanged | Plain, and the failure says what to do. |
| After the list | Lines not listed are fine as written. | unchanged | |
| Fit, the level | How the page reads: Senior product manager | **Level a recruiter would assume: Senior product manager** | "The page reads" is on the voice guide's translate list. |
| Fit, the posting gap | What the posting asks for that the page doesn't show | **What the posting asks for that your resume doesn't show** | "The page" again; it is their resume. |
| The score note | What the rung and the number mean / The rung is what a recruiter does with the page after one pass. The number says where in the rung it sits. It doesn't predict interviews or offers. | **What the score means** / **The band is what a recruiter would do with your resume after one pass. The number says where in the band you sit. Neither predicts interviews or offers.** | "Rung" was a ladder metaphor the reader had to translate; the product has called these bands since November 1 and the copy system records them as bands. |
| The employer lift | A recent employer whose hiring bar recruiters know on sight lifted this page one rung. The lines under that name still get their cards. | **A recent employer recruiters know on sight moved this resume up one band. The lines under that name still need the work.** | Plainer, and "cards" is ours, not theirs. |
| The re-check, clean twice | Still nothing that needs work. / Both reads came back clean. | Still nothing that needs work. / **Both versions came back clean.** | "Reads" is on the translate list. |

Start with the first one again. | The same lines are on the list. / **This version didn't clear any of them. Start with the first again.** | "Clear" says what happened to a line; "move" didn't. |
| The re-check, all gone | Every line you worked on is off the list. / Nothing on this page needs a card now. | Every line you worked on is off the list. / **Nothing in this version needs work now.** | "Page" and "card" are ours. |
| The PDF | The same headings and labels | Follows the page | One name everywhere. |
| Methodology, the bands | One of four rungs… The rung is what a recruiter does with the page… / …lift the page one rung | **One of four bands… The band is what a recruiter would do with the resume…** / **…lift the resume one band** | The public page says what the report says. The section heading "Line by line." becomes "Lines that need work." |

Not changed, on purpose: the band names and their anchor sentences (Matt's, November 5), the ending's lines ("Made your edits? See what changed.", "Send it. Then read it against the posting you're going for.", "Your line is ready. Drop it into your resume and see what changed."), the wait screen's stages, and "I don't know yet".
