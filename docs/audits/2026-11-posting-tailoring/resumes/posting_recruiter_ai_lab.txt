Technical Recruiter
Remote-friendly (travel required) | Denver, CO | Seattle, WA | New York City, NY

About Northlight

Northlight builds AI systems that people can rely on. We are a fast-growing group of researchers, engineers and operators.

About the role

As a Technical Recruiter you will find, engage and hire people across our technical teams, with a focus on the security organization as it grows.

Key responsibilities
Build and run recruiting plans to find and source strong candidates
Partner with hiring managers and interviewers to understand hiring needs and the skills each role requires
Run outreach campaigns and build relationships with leaders in the field
Work with leadership on long-term hiring plans across technical teams
Give every candidate a good experience from first contact to offer

Minimum qualifications
Deep full lifecycle recruiting experience, with substantial time supporting technical engineering organizations
Proficiency with an applicant tracking system such as Greenhouse and with modern sourcing tools
Experience partnering directly with hiring managers on intake, bar calibration and interview loop design
Sound independent judgment on candidate quality
Genuine interest in Northlight's mission and in the part a strong security function plays in it

Preferred qualifications
Experience recruiting at a high-growth technology, AI or machine learning company
Working knowledge of security credentials and where they do and do not carry signal
Experience hiring for security engineering, incident response, threat intelligence or physical security
Experience improving recruiting processes or interview design where they were thin or absent
Comfort using LLMs to speed up sourcing, research and market mapping

Annual salary: $210,000 - $260,000 USD

Logistics

Minimum education: Bachelor's degree or an equivalent combination of education, training and experience
Required field of study: A field relevant to the role, as shown through coursework, training or professional experience
Minimum years of experience: Years of experience will correlate with the internal job level for the position
Location-based hybrid policy: we expect all staff to be in one of our offices at least 25% of the time
Visa sponsorship: we sponsor visas, though not for every role and every candidate

We encourage you to apply even if you do not meet every qualification. Northlight is an equal opportunity employer and considers all qualified applicants, including those with arrest or conviction records, in line with applicable law.
