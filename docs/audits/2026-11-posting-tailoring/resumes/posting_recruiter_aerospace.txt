Technical Recruiter, Security
Waco, TX

Meridian Launch builds reusable rockets and the satellites they carry. We are hiring across every corporate function to keep up with our launch schedule.

TECHNICAL RECRUITER, SECURITY

As a Meridian recruiter you will run a full desk: sourcing, screening, interviewing and closing candidates for our corporate teams, including IT, application software, physical security and information security. You are often a candidate's first contact with the company. We work in teams, and we expect you to take a search from intake to signed offer without waiting to be told the next step.

RESPONSIBILITIES:

Meet with your hiring teams to agree headcount, new requisitions, hiring plans and interview feedback
Screen candidates who apply to open requisitions
Source candidates who are not looking
Recommend the strongest candidates to your hiring teams for interviews
Sit in on interviews as another evaluator
Run the offer process: background checks, negotiation and the offer letter

BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:

Bachelor's degree and 1+ years of recruiting experience; or 3+ years of professional experience as a recruiter in lieu of a degree

PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:

2+ years of technical recruiting experience
2+ years of experience recruiting for IT, information security, software engineering or physical security
Experience with in-house recruiting
Experience with high volume recruiting
Experience recruiting skilled labor
Full life cycle recruiting experience, from sourcing to closing
Comfort reviewing and reporting on recruiting data
Demonstrated expertise in non-standard recruiting methods: cold calling, ad hoc networking events, targeted sourcing campaigns
Comfortable working directly with hiring managers, including director and VP level technical leaders
Ability to multi-task and operate with little direction

ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:

Available to work extended hours and weekends as needed
This position is based in Long Beach, CA, and requires being onsite full-time; hybrid and remote work will not be considered

EXPORT CONTROL REQUIREMENTS:

To conform to U.S. Government export regulations, applicant must be a (i) U.S. citizen or national, (ii) U.S. lawful permanent resident, (iii) refugee, or (iv) asylee, or be eligible to obtain the required authorizations from the U.S. Department of State.
