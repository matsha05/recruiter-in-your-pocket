Registered Nurse, Medical-Surgical Unit (Nights)
Triangle Regional Medical Center, Raleigh, NC

About the role
We are hiring a Registered Nurse for our 36-bed medical-surgical unit, night shift (7p to 7a), three 12-hour shifts per week with rotating weekends.

Requirements
- Current unrestricted North Carolina RN license or compact state license
- BLS certification required; ACLS within six months of hire
- Minimum 1 year of acute care nursing experience preferred; new graduates with a strong clinical rotation considered
- Experience with Epic or a comparable EHR
- Ability to manage a patient assignment of 5 to 6 patients
- Strong communication with patients, families, and the interdisciplinary team

Responsibilities
- Assess, plan, implement, and evaluate patient care in collaboration with physicians and the care team
- Administer medications and treatments per orders and unit protocol
- Document care accurately and in a timely manner in the EHR
- Educate patients and families on conditions, medications, and discharge plans
- Precept students and new graduates as assigned
