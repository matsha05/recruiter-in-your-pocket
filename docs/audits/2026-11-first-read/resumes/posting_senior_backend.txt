Senior Backend Engineer, Money Movement
Cobalt Pay (payments infrastructure, Series D), Seattle or Remote (US)

About the role
You'll own services that move money: payouts, refunds and the ledger behind them. You'll design for correctness first, run what you build, and raise the bar for reliability across the team.

What you'll do
- Design and build high-throughput, idempotent payment services in Go
- Own the reliability of the payouts path end to end, including on-call
- Lead technical design reviews and mentor engineers on the team
- Partner with Finance and Risk on reconciliation and controls

What we're looking for
- 5+ years building backend systems, ideally in payments or financial services
- Strong Go or Java, and deep experience with PostgreSQL
- Experience with event-driven systems such as Kafka
- A record of leading migrations or reliability work on production systems
- Bonus: experience with ledgers, double-entry accounting or settlement
