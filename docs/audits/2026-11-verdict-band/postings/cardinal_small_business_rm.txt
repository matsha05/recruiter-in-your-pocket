Small Business Relationship Manager
Cardinal Southern Bank, Birmingham, AL (hybrid)

Cardinal Southern Bank is looking for a Small Business Relationship Manager to build and keep a portfolio of business clients with revenue under $5M.

Responsibilities
- Bring in new small business clients and grow deposits and loans across an assigned portfolio
- Structure and present small business loans and lines of credit with our credit team
- Meet clients on site and in our branches; partner with branch managers on referrals
- Keep client files complete for credit review and audit

Qualifications
- 4+ years in business banking, commercial lending or branch management with a lending focus
- Experience originating small business loans or lines of credit
- A track record of growing a deposit or loan portfolio
- Bachelor's degree in finance, accounting or business
- Strong communication skills

Nice to have
- SBA lending experience
- Formal credit training

Hybrid: three days in our downtown office. Some travel within the Birmingham metro. Cardinal Southern is an equal opportunity employer.
