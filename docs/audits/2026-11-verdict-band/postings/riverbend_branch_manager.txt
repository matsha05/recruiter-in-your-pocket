Branch Manager, Retail Banking
Riverbend Federal Credit Union, Birmingham, AL (on site)

About the role
Riverbend Federal Credit Union is hiring a Branch Manager to lead our Five Points South branch, serving about 9,000 members. You will run daily operations, grow deposits and loans, and coach a team of 8 to 12.

What you'll do
- Lead and develop branch staff, including tellers, member service representatives and a loan officer
- Grow deposit and consumer loan balances against quarterly goals
- Keep the branch audit-ready and compliant with BSA/AML, Reg CC and NCUA requirements
- Resolve escalated member concerns
- Partner with the regional team on community outreach and small business relationships

Required qualifications
- 5+ years of retail banking or credit union experience, including 3+ years managing a branch or a team of at least 6
- A record of growing deposits or loans at the branch level
- Working knowledge of BSA/AML and branch compliance and audit procedures
- Bachelor's degree in finance, business or a related field, or equivalent experience

Preferred qualifications
- Experience with consumer and small business lending
- NMLS registration, or the ability to register within 90 days
- Experience in a credit union or community bank

This is an on-site role, Monday to Friday with some Saturdays. A background and credit check is required.

Pay: $72,000 to $88,000 a year, plus an annual incentive. Riverbend is an equal opportunity employer.
